Spacewrite

Privacy Policy

Effective 3 October 2026

Spacewrite is a word game made by Wisam Abbod. This page explains, in plain words, what the game stores, what it sends to a server, and what it does not do.

The short version

  • There is no account. The game never asks for your email, phone number, real name, contacts, photos or location.
  • There are no adverts, and no advertising or analytics trackers of any kind.
  • Your progress lives on your device. Only what is needed for the global leaderboard and for online games is sent to a server.
  • Nothing is sold or shared with anyone for marketing.

What stays on your device

Your settings, statistics, streak, puzzle history, stardust balance and the items you have unlocked are stored only on your device. Deleting the app, or using Settings → Reset all data, removes them.

What is sent to the server

The global leaderboard and online games need a server. When you use them, the game sends:

  • An anonymous player ID. A random identifier created automatically on your device. It is not linked to your name, email, Apple ID or device identifiers.
  • Your display name, if you chose one. Other players can see it on the leaderboard and in online games, so do not use a name you want kept private. You can change it or leave it blank (“Anonymous”) at any time.
  • Daily puzzle results: your score, number of words, best word and the date of the puzzle.
  • Online game data: the rooms you join, the moves you play and the final results of those games.
  • Reports. If you report another player's name, the report (your anonymous ID, the reported player's anonymous ID and name) is stored so it can be reviewed.

As with any internet service, the server's hosting provider also processes technical information such as your IP address in order to deliver responses and keep the service secure. The game does not use it to identify you.

Where it is stored

Server data is held with Supabase, in a data centre in Frankfurt, Germany (European Union). Supabase acts only as a hosting provider.

How it is used

Only to run the game: to show the leaderboard, to let you play with other people, and to keep names appropriate. It is never used for advertising or profiling, and it is not sold or passed to third parties.

How long it is kept

  • Leaderboard scores are kept so that past puzzles keep their leaderboards.
  • Abandoned online games are deleted automatically after about a week.
  • Reports are kept for as long as they are needed for moderation.

Your choices

  • You can play the daily puzzle, practice and offline mode without a display name.
  • You can hide other players, and report offensive names, by tapping a name on the leaderboard or in an online game.
  • You can ask for the server data linked to your player ID to be deleted. Contact us (see below) with your display name and the date of a puzzle you played, so the right records can be found.

Children

Spacewrite does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children. Because display names are visible to other players, children should choose a name that does not identify them.

Changes
