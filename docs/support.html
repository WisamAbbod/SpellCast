Support

Get in touch

Found a bug, have a question, or want your data deleted? Open a support request and describe what happened. Mention your device and, for a problem in a game, roughly when it was.

Common questions

How do I play?

Swipe across neighbouring letters to spell a word. Tap the ? on the menu for the full rules of each mode.

I lost my streak or my stardust

Progress is stored on your device, not in an account. Deleting the app or resetting your data removes it, and it cannot be restored.

Online play or the leaderboard is not working

Both need an internet connection. Check your connection and try again; the daily puzzle, practice and offline mode work without one.

Someone has an offensive name

Tap their name on the leaderboard, in an online lobby or on the results screen, then choose Report name or Hide this player. Hidden players can be shown again in Settings.

How do I change my name?

Settings → Your name. Leave it blank to appear as “Anonymous”.

How do I delete my data?

On your device: Settings → Reset all data. For leaderboard scores and online game records held on the server, open a support request with your display name and the date of a puzzle you played. See the privacy policy for what is stored.
